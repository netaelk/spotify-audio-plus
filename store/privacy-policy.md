# Privacy Policy: Spotify Audio+

Last updated: 2026-10-11

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

- It runs only on https://open.spotify.com/ and only modifies the player page you are viewing (adds buttons and a loop highlight).
- It reads the current playback position and track shown on that page solely to make its features work. This information stays in your browser and is discarded when the page closes.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking, and does not use cookies or browser storage.
- It is not affiliated with Spotify AB.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/netaelk/spotify-audio-plus/issues
