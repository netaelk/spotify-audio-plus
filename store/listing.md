## Category
Entertainment (or Productivity)

## Language
English

## Description
Adds two practical controls to the Spotify web player (open.spotify.com):

SKIP BUTTONS
• Jump back 10 / 5 seconds and forward 5 / 10 seconds with buttons placed on both sides of play/pause.

A-B LOOP
• A new button next to the built-in repeat button.
• Click it, then pick the start point (A) and end point (B) by clicking the progress bar, or by clicking the button while the song is at the right moment.
• The looped section is highlighted on the progress bar: light green for the part already played, dark green for the part still to come.
• Click the button again (or press Esc while selecting) to cancel. The loop resets automatically when the track changes.

Great for learning a song by ear, practicing an instrument, studying lyrics, or replaying a favorite moment.

PRIVACY
• No data is collected, stored or sent anywhere. The extension only runs on open.spotify.com and only changes the page you are viewing.
• Requests no browser permissions.
